Privacy Policy

Digital Tasbeeh: Dhikr Counter · Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how the Digital Tasbeeh app ("the app") handles information. In short: the app does not collect, send or sell your personal data.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your counts, chosen dhikr, targets, language and vibration setting are saved only on your own device (in the app's local storage) so the app can remember them. This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes it.

Permissions

The app may use your device's vibration feature when you tap the counter. It does not need access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

Advertising and purchases

The current version contains no advertising. If optional purchases or ads are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before they are released, and any payments will be handled by Google Play.

Third-party services

The app works offline. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not have access to or use that information.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
